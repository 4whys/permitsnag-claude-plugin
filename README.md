# PermitSnag

Ask Claude about US backcountry permits and campgrounds. The PermitSnag connector looks up how a permit is issued, its season and published dates, and what PermitSnag's checks last recorded for your dates, with the time of the check. The skill tells Claude to state that age, to treat unknown dates as unknown, and to hand you to the official booking page.

## Data

The plugin sends the permit or campground name and dates you ask about to api.permitsnag.com. There is no account and no sign-in. PermitSnag does not receive your conversation. Privacy policy: https://permitsnag.com/privacy

## More

Setup steps for other assistants and the public server contract: https://permitsnag.com/connect and https://permitsnag.com/developers
